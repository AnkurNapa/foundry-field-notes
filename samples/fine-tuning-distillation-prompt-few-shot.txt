Route the Harbour Goods support message to one queue: returns, delivery, warranty, billing, store, escalate. Reply with the queue name only.

Examples:
Message: I want to send back the kettle, it is faulty
Queue: returns

Message: Return request for kettle, bought 5 days ago
Queue: returns

Message: Where is my kettle? It was due 5 days ago
Queue: delivery

Message: Courier did not come for the kettle
Queue: delivery

Message: The kettle stopped working after 5 months
Queue: warranty

Message: Is my kettle still under warranty?
Queue: warranty

Message: I was charged twice for the kettle
Queue: billing

Message: Invoice for the kettle shows the wrong GST
Queue: billing

Message: Is the kettle in stock at ST-011?
Queue: store

Message: What time does ST-003 close?
Queue: store

Message: This is the third time I am writing about the kettle
Queue: escalate

Message: I will go to consumer court over the kettle
Queue: escalate
