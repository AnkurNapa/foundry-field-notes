# Harbour Goods customer policies (synthetic sample)

Synthetic data for Foundry Field Notes. Harbour Goods is a fictional retailer.

## Returns policy

Harbour Goods accepts returns within 30 days of delivery when the customer has the receipt or the order ID. Order IDs look like HG-240318-0042. Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of the returned item reaching the store. Returns are accepted at any of our six stores or by courier pickup. Opened software, gift cards and personal care items cannot be returned.

## Delivery promise

Standard delivery takes 3 working days in metro cities and 5 working days elsewhere. Metro cities are Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi and Kolkata. Pune is served from store ST-014 Pune West and follows the 5 working day promise. Orders placed after 6 PM IST count from the next working day. Delivery is free on orders of INR 999 or more; below that a charge of INR 79 applies.

## Electronics warranty

Electronics carry a 12 month warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage or damage from unauthorised repair. To claim, the customer brings the item and the order ID to any store. The store sends the item for inspection and replies within 10 working days.

## Support hours and stores

Customer support is open from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, seven days a week. Our stores are ST-003 Bengaluru South, ST-007 Chennai South, ST-011 Mumbai West, ST-014 Pune West, ST-019 Delhi North and ST-022 Kolkata East. Any other store code, such as ST-099, is not a Harbour Goods store.
