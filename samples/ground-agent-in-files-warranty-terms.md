# Harbour Goods warranty terms

Policy ID: HG-POL-WAR. Version 2.1. Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Product Quality.

## W1. Warranty periods

Electronics carry a 12-month warranty from the invoice date. Furniture carries a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Kitchen appliances carry the electronics warranty of 12 months.

## W2. What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures under normal household use. Harbour Goods will repair the product first. If a repair is not possible within 15 working days, the product is replaced with the same or an equivalent model.

## W3. What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, power surges, commercial use, normal wear of batteries and cushions, or products repaired by anyone other than an authorised service partner.

## W4. Making a warranty claim

Customers claim by visiting any of the six stores or by calling support between 9 AM and 9 PM IST. They need the order ID or the invoice. A claim after the 30-day return window is handled under this warranty, not under the returns policy.

## W5. Extended warranty

Customers can buy a 24-month extended warranty on electronics within 30 days of purchase. It costs 8 per cent of the product price, with a minimum of INR 499.
