# Harbour Goods delivery promise

Policy ID: HG-POL-DEL. Version 3.0. Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Logistics.

## D1. Standard delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 working days in metro cities and 5 working days everywhere else. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays. The clock starts on the first working day after the order is confirmed.

## D2. Which cities count as metro

For this promise, metro cities are Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and Pune. All other delivery addresses, including Jaipur, use the 5 working day promise.

## D3. Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders of INR 2,000 or more. Orders below INR 2,000 carry a delivery charge of INR 99. Large furniture carries a flat charge of INR 499 regardless of order value.

## D4. Late deliveries

If an order arrives after the promised date, the customer can claim a INR 150 voucher by quoting the order ID to support. The voucher is valid for 90 days. Delays caused by an incorrect address or a missed delivery slot do not qualify.

## D5. Tracking an order

Customers receive a tracking link by SMS and email when the order leaves the warehouse. Support can also look up any order by its order ID between 9 AM and 9 PM IST.

## D6. Store pickup

Customers can choose free pickup from any of the six stores. Pickup orders are ready within 2 working days and are held for 7 days before they are cancelled and refunded.
