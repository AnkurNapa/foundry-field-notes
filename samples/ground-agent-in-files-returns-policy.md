# Harbour Goods returns policy

Policy ID: HG-POL-RET. Version 4.2. Effective 1 July 2026. Owner: Customer Support.

## R1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers products bought from Harbour Goods stores and from harbourgoods.example online. It applies to all six stores: ST-003 Bengaluru South, ST-007 Chennai South, ST-011 Mumbai West, ST-014 Pune West, ST-019 Delhi North and ST-022 Kolkata East.

## R2. The 30-day rule

Customers can return most products within 30 days of the delivery date with the receipt or the order ID. Order IDs look like HG-240318-0042. Day 1 is the day after delivery. A return on day 31 or later is not accepted under this policy. The warranty policy may still apply.

## R3. Condition of returned products

Products must be unused and in the original packaging with all accessories. Opened electronics can be returned within the 30 days only if the seal on the inner box is intact or the product is faulty on arrival.

## R4. Products that cannot be returned

The following cannot be returned: gift cards, opened personal care products, clearance items marked "Final sale" on the receipt and made-to-measure curtains and blinds.

## R5. Returns without a receipt

Without a receipt or an order ID, Harbour Goods offers an exchange or store credit only. Store credit is valid for 12 months. No cash or card refund is given without proof of purchase.

## R6. Refund method and timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card and UPI refunds are issued within 7 working days of the return being accepted. Cash purchases are refunded in cash at the store. Delivery charges are refunded only if the product was faulty or wrong.

## R7. How to start a return

Bring the product to any of the six stores, or start a return online from the order page. Online returns are collected from the delivery address within 3 working days in metro cities and 5 working days elsewhere. Support is open 9 AM to 9 PM IST, seven days a week.
