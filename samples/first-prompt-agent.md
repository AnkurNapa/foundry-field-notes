# Harbour Goods customer policies

Synthetic sample for Foundry Field Notes. Harbour Goods is a fictional retailer.

## Returns policy

- We accept returns within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt or order confirmation.
- Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of the return being received.
- Without a receipt we can't process a return.

## Delivery promise

- Standard delivery takes 3 working days in metro cities and 5 working days elsewhere.
- Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.
- If an order is late, the customer can ask support to raise a delivery query. Support quotes the order ID, which looks like HG-240318-0042.

## Electronics warranty

- Electronics carry a 12 month warranty from the date of delivery.
- The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It doesn't cover accidental damage or water damage.
- Customers raise a warranty claim with the order ID and a short description of the fault.

## Support hours

- Support is open 9 AM to 9 PM IST, seven days a week.
- Outside those hours, customers can leave a message and support replies the next working day.
