Why this site exists

I started on the brewhouse floor, then worked across distilling and winemaking. Somewhere between a stuck ferment and a spreadsheet nobody trusted, I started building the data side myself. That led to data platforms, the DP-600 certification and, more and more, AI apps and agents.

Foundry changes every month. Names change, features move from preview to general availability and old ones retire. I wanted one roadmap that says what to learn in what order, checked against Microsoft Learn, with the date it was checked on every page.

I am learning Foundry in public. I do not hold AI-103 yet. The site says so on every walkthrough I have not run end to end myself. When I pass it, or get something wrong, you will read it here first.

How the notes are written

  • Every walkthrough says which month of Foundry it describes. It also says whether I have run it or checked it against Microsoft Learn.
  • Product facts come from Microsoft Learn. When I disagree with the documentation, I say so and show why.
  • Examples use a fictional retailer called Harbour Goods with synthetic data. Nothing here comes from a client.
  • When I get something wrong, I correct it in public.

The journey

Foundry's milestones and mine, on the dates official pages confirm. Where only the month is confirmed, only the month is shown.

  1. 12 October 2026

    Foundry Field Notes begins

    This site: the same roadmap format, built for Microsoft Foundry.

  2. 24 September 2026

    GenAI series on the blog

    Agents, tools, retrieval and evaluation, worked through on brewing and distilling problems.

  3. 30 June 2026

    AI-102 retires, AI-103 takes over

    Exam AI-103, Developing AI Apps and Agents on Azure, replaces AI-102 and is built around Foundry.

  4. 18 November 2025

    Microsoft Foundry at Ignite 2025

    Announced under the Microsoft Foundry name, with Foundry IQ and the Foundry Control Plane in preview.

  5. May 2025

    Agent Service reaches general availability

    Agents with tools, knowledge and threads become a supported production service.

  6. 19 November 2024

    Azure AI Studio becomes Azure AI Foundry

    Announced at Microsoft Ignite 2024, together with the Azure AI Foundry SDK.

  7. November 2023

    Azure AI Studio enters preview

    Announced at Microsoft Ignite 2023: one place to build generative AI apps on Azure.
