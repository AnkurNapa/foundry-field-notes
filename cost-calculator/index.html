Token cost calculator

A Foundry model bills for the tokens each call reads and writes. Put in your traffic and your prices. Every line shows its formula so you can defend the number in front of a finance team.

Prices checked 12 October 2026, Global Standard, check your region. They come from the Azure Retail Prices API, which feeds the Azure OpenAI pricing page. Data Zone deployments cost about 10% more. Your agreement and taxes will differ. Use this to compare options, not as a quote.
