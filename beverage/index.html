Foundry on the brewhouse floor

I came to AI from beer, then whisky and wine. In a plant an assistant that guesses is a liability, so every pattern here keeps the numbers in tools, the answers grounded and a person in charge. This is the roadmap seen from the floor: the gap in each trade and the Foundry pattern that fits it.

Beer and malting

A brewery answers the same questions every week: why does this batch taste different, which complaint matters, what does the spec say. The answers sit in SOPs, lab sheets and batch records that nobody has time to search.

Speak the units: Degrees Plato, hectolitres, EBC colour, IBU, apparent attenuation.

On the floorIn FoundryLearn it
Triage consumer complaints by likely cause and urgency A prompt agent with structured output that tags each complaint with a fault class, batch code and severity, plus an evaluation set written by the quality team
  • Level 2: Prompts and structured outputs
  • Level 2: Custom evaluators and test sets
Investigate an off-flavour across batch records and lab results An agent with read-only tools over batch and lab data, grounded in the flavour standards, that cites every record it used
  • Level 1: Give an agent built-in tools
  • Level 2: Foundry IQ knowledge bases
Answer brewing maths without the model doing sums Function or MCP tools for gravity, ABV and bitterness, so the model calls a calculation instead of guessing it
  • Level 2: Toolboxes and MCP servers
  • Level 2: Prompts and structured outputs
Put the SOP library in front of every shift A Foundry IQ knowledge base over controlled SOPs, with citations and a refusal when the SOP does not cover it
  • Level 2: Foundry IQ knowledge bases
  • Level 2: Prompt shields and groundedness

Whisky and spirits

Spirits run on long timelines and tight rules: litres of pure alcohol, cask records, bonded stock. An assistant that guesses a number or touches a setpoint is worse than none.

Speak the units: Litres of pure alcohol, ABV, cut points, cask age.

On the floorIn FoundryLearn it
Give the still operator cited answers, never control An agent grounded in SOPs and run logs with read-only tools, human approval for anything that changes a record, no write access to the plant
  • Level 1: Ground an agent in your files
  • Level 3: Long-running agents and human approval
Write the shift handover from the logs A prompt with a fixed structured output, evaluated against handovers the team wrote by hand
  • Level 2: Prompts and structured outputs
  • Level 1: Run your first evaluation
Keep an assistant from inventing cask history Groundedness evaluation and prompt shields on every release, red teaming before go-live
  • Level 2: Prompt shields and groundedness
  • Level 3: AI red teaming

Wine

Wine runs on vintage time and a chain from block to tank to barrel to blend. The knowledge is in tasting notes, lab reports and the head of the winemaker. It leaves when people do.

Speak the units: Brix, YAN, volatile acidity, free SO2, litres per tonne.

On the floorIn FoundryLearn it
Pull results out of lab certificates and analysis reports Content Understanding to turn PDFs into fields with confidence scores, low-confidence fields sent to a person
  • Level 2: Extract structure with Content Understanding
  • Level 3: Long-running agents and human approval
Search years of tasting and cellar notes in plain language Hybrid search with semantic ranking over the notes, every answer linked to its source note
  • Level 1: Your first Azure AI Search index
  • Level 3: Retrieval quality
Trace a bottle through blend and barrel when a question comes in An agent with a lot genealogy tool, so the chain comes from the system of record and not from the model
  • Level 1: Give an agent built-in tools
  • Level 2: Toolboxes and MCP servers

Food, beverage and alcobev

Across food and alcobev the hard questions repeat: where did this lot go, why did demand move, what does this supplier document say. Most answers sit in systems and documents that were never joined.

Speak the units: Lots and batches, cases, excise duty by state, depletions, shelf life.

On the floorIn FoundryLearn it
Give sales teams a forecasting assistant they can question An agent that calls the forecast model and the sales data as tools and explains the drivers, with numbers only from tools
  • Level 2: Hosted agents with Agent Framework
  • Level 1: A chat app on your own data
Read supplier certificates and specifications at intake Content Understanding analysers per document type, results checked against the specification before acceptance
  • Level 2: Extract structure with Content Understanding
  • Level 2: Custom evaluators and test sets
Answer customer and distributor questions in their language A grounded support agent with content filters, published to the channel people already use
  • Level 1: Content filters and blocklists
  • Level 3: Publish to Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot
Keep AI costs predictable through festival peaks Token budgets per app, the right deployment type and a cost alert, measured before the peak
  • Level 2: Deployment types and quota
  • Level 3: Cost control
